FoodVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

What we collect

  • Account info — when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We never see your password.
  • Your content — recipes you save (including links you paste), meal plans, shopping lists, and notes.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: store your recipes, build meal plans, and generate shopping lists.
  • Recipe links and text you submit are processed by AI language models to extract ingredients, instructions, and nutrition estimates. AI output may be inaccurate — always use your own judgment, especially for allergies and dietary needs.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, where your account and content are stored.
  • AI providers — recipe text is sent to third-party AI models for extraction. Only recipe content is sent, never your identity.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all your data at any time from inside the app: tap your profile picture → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
