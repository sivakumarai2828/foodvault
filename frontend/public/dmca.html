Content & Copyright Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Our approach

FoodVault respects the rights of content creators. Recipes are saved by individual users from links or text they choose. For each saved recipe, FoodVault stores factual details (ingredients, steps, nutrition estimates), generates its own illustrative image, and keeps a link back to the original source. FoodVault does not copy or re-host creators' photos or videos, and does not display another user's saved recipes publicly.

If you are a rights holder

If you believe factual content associated with your work appears in the app in a way that infringes your rights, contact us and we will review and, where appropriate, remove it promptly.

Send a notice to aichatspace28@gmail.com including:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Identification of the work you believe is infringed.
  • The specific recipe, link, or content in the app you want addressed (a screenshot or the source URL helps).
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by you, your agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notice

If content you added was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same address with your contact details, identification of the removed content, and a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief it was removed in error.

Repeat infringers

We may suspend or terminate the accounts of users who repeatedly add infringing content.
